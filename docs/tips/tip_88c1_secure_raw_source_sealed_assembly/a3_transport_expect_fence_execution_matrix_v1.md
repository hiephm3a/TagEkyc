## Homeowner contract decision and design hold

The Homeowner confirmed that raw payload length **1 byte and above remains size-valid** subject to every other admission rule. F1 at lengths 1–1024 is therefore `REAL_PRODUCT_BLOCKER`, not an undersized-test exclusion. Raising a minimum to 1025, allowing chunked requests, or claiming a proxy topology is outside this correction. This is a design checkpoint only; no transport product code has been changed. The Agent raw transport must not invoke body copying until an authenticated direct-Kestrel HTTP `100 Continue` is received after durable B/R1. Any final response or overall deadline before `100` must close without touching the raw lease. A narrow one-request strict-Continue transport is the proposed direction, subject to design review before implementation.
